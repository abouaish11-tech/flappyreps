      How it works & FAQ ↓

      What is Flappy Reps?

      Flappy Reps is a free-to-try browser game where your body is the controller. It's Flappy Bird, but instead of tapping you do push-ups, squats or a plank in front of your phone or laptop camera. Go down and the bird drops, come up and it rises through the pipes. No equipment, no download, no account.

      Three workout modes

      • Push-ups: the camera follows your shoulders. Phone on the floor, facing you.
      • Squats: the camera follows your hips. Stand back until your hips are in frame.
      • Plank: hold still and keep the bird level. Your score is the seconds you hold.

      Private by design

      Body tracking runs on your own device with Google's MediaPipe Pose model. Your camera video is never uploaded.

      FAQ

      Is there a game you control with push-ups?

      Yes. Flappy Reps is a Flappy Bird style game in your browser where push-ups control the bird. Your camera tracks your shoulders: go down and the bird drops, push up and it rises through the pipes. It also has squat and plank modes.

      How does Flappy Reps track my body?

      It runs Google's MediaPipe Pose model inside your browser to find your shoulders (push-ups) or hips (squats and plank) in each camera frame, then maps their height onto the bird.

      Is my camera video uploaded anywhere?

      No. The video is processed on your own device and never leaves it. There is no account and nothing to install.

      Is Flappy Reps free?

      Your first run each day is free. For unlimited play there is a $1.99 Day Pass (24 hours) or Pro at $4.99 per month, cancel anytime.

      What do I need to play?

      A phone or laptop with a camera and a modern browser. No equipment, no app download, no account. Open flappyreps.com, allow the camera and get in position.

      Where should I put my phone?

      For push-ups, put it on the floor in front of you, facing you. For squats, stand back until your hips are in frame. For plank, put it at your side so your whole body is in frame.
